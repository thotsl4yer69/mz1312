THE INVESTIGATION DESK. Practical clinic: factual investigations, official notes, interviews and reports.

Several units work from the same investigative source trail but require different skills. CPPINV3030 is about conducting factual enquiries, rather than merely planning them. The national performance evidence identifies three factual investigation types: general insurance, compulsory third party liability and workers compensation. For each, collect authorised facts, examine records, interview relevant people and test contradictions. Keep original source materials, source limitations, chronology and a documented end-of-work review. Do not present an expert medical opinion unless appropriately instructed and qualified.

PSPCRT029 concerns official notes. Make accurate, contemporaneous entries showing dates, times, observations, decisions and relevant reasons. Preserve corrections and identify derived copies. Then demonstrate how original notes support a later statement, incident report or testimony. Do not quietly replace handwritten notes with a clean typed narrative that adds information never recorded at the time. The archived outline under the current folder name belongs to an older unit; verify the current PSPCRT029 standard.

CPPINV3031 addresses interviews and statements. Plan interviews with the proper purpose, authorisation, neutral questions, accessibility and support where needed, and lawful recording. Let a witness describe what they actually saw before testing specifics. Where they heard information second-hand, record that distinction. Create statements that the witness genuinely reviews or adopts under the applicable procedure. An unsigned account's status must be clear; do not manufacture agreement.

PSPREG035 is the formal record-of-interview unit. It is not a private interview renamed with an official-looking form. The national standard requires two formal interviews. Each must have documented preparation, a suitable interview setting, applicable rights and safeguards, questioning, changes in approach if required, exhibits, interruptions and a complete formal record. Do not copy police or regulator compulsory powers into a private investigator's voluntary interview. The older course outline in that unit folder was actually labelled PSPREG407B and cannot replace the modern requirements.

CPPINV3032 concerns factual reports. Three reports are required across specified case categories: workers compensation, general insurance and a qualifying third category such as CTP liability, financial crime or internal discipline. Each report connects instructions to activities, then to correctly attributed evidence and qualified findings. A supplied photograph taken days after an incident cannot be said to prove conditions at the incident time. Preserve contradictory accounts, correct timestamps and identify unavailable sources. Attach actual referenced exhibits and check all calculations.

To build a useful practice portfolio, organise separate authorised case files for general insurance, CTP and workers compensation. Add each source as it is lawfully received. Record who conducted each interview and when. Produce appropriate original notes, formal interview records and case-specific reports. Check where the assessor allows evidence to serve several units; overlap must be authentic and demonstrate all requirements rather than duplicate a made-up interview.

Your final self-test is whether an independent authorised reviewer can answer what was personally done, what the source demonstrates, what remains uncertain, which rights were observed and which required assessed performances have actually occurred. Never mark a practical performance complete because you can recite how to do it.
