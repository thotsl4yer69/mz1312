THE INVESTIGATION DESK. Final qualification roadmap.

You have reached the end of the original seventy-one audio episodes and the targeted repair lessons. Here is how the teaching connects to the actual qualification.

CPP30619 Certificate Three in Investigative Services requires sixteen units: five core and eleven electives. Your archived Drive course has eighteen unit folders, so some may be optional or overlapping. Do not assume that every archived unit is an additional requirement. Confirm the actual selected eleven electives with the Academy before beginning optional assessments.

The Academy's trainer instructed you to complete the five foundation units in this order: CPPINV3026 working effectively in investigative services; CPPINV3027 developing investigation plans; CPPINV3028 investigating and locating subjects; CPPINV3029 providing quality investigative services to clients; and CPPSEC3124 preparing and presenting evidence in court. Audio numbers do not follow this order because Episode Two belongs to the planning unit and Episodes Thirty-six to Thirty-nine cover court evidence.

For the first unit, gather the required three case types with case-specific legal and ethical work, including two-jurisdiction legislative application. For the second, build two specifically categorised factual plans and one surveillance plan. For the third, demonstrate the required work involving three different subjects. For the fourth, provide services to three different clients: workers compensation, general insurance and compulsory third party liability. For the court unit, prepare two different proceedings and satisfy the direct-assessment observation requirement. The original Giving Evidence videos and forms must still be used.

Your elective workstreams extend factual investigations, official notes, interviews, formal interview records, reporting, work health and safety, sensitive information, surveillance operations and vehicles, legal process serving and establishing a compliant business. A polished hypothetical report is not proof that an interview, surveillance operation or service of documents actually took place.

Keep one evidence checklist for every selected unit. Record the exact current Academy requirement, its source, the matching lesson or resource, your own actual work or expressly approved simulated performance, the resulting evidence file and the assessor's outcome. If a field is not verified, leave it incomplete. Source documents and original audio can teach the skill, but cannot substitute for original videos or witnessed practical activity.

For every investigation, start by identifying the client's legitimate instruction and the scope of lawful authority. Check which laws apply. Record what is known, missing and merely alleged. Choose methods that test competing explanations without creating unnecessary danger or intrusion. Preserve original source records, time accuracy and handling continuity. Write qualified conclusions and identify what remains unresolved. Securely hand over the genuine final result.

A final practice question: could another authorised investigator pick up your file and reconstruct the work from its source records, permissions, decisions and outcomes? If not, improve the file before closing it.

The Investigation Desk is an educational companion. Your qualification is earned through the Academy's actual training plan, personal assessments and competency decisions. The goal is not just to have listened to every recording. It is to demonstrate investigative work that is safe, accurate, lawful and independently verifiable.
