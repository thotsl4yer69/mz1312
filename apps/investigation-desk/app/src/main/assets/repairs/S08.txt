THE INVESTIGATION DESK. Practical clinic: health and safety, sensitive information, process serving and business compliance.

HLTWHS003 requires more than recognising a hazard on paper. Conduct and record a risk assessment and residual controls. Communicate safety information to at least two workers or approved training participants, check the correct protective equipment and housekeeping arrangements, monitor safety practices and complete an actual or authorised simulated incident and emergency exercise. Separate work you personally carried out from something merely proposed. A plan for a drill does not demonstrate that a drill occurred.

PSPSEC024 concerns handling sensitive information. Across two approved occasions, follow applicable government security rules, classification and secure receipt, transmission, access, retention and disposal practices. Seek clarification from the person authorised to classify, transmit or advise on the material. A government classification mark cannot be invented to make a practice packet look realistic. Preserve receipt, handling and authorised decision records. The exact security classification system depends on the relevant organisation and governing policy.

FNSMCA313 covers serving legal process. The national performance evidence requires service on at least three occasions, using at least two different types of permitted service. For each, identify the actual court, documents, recipient, authorised procedure, deadline, lawful access and proof that the service or attempt occurred. An unsuccessful attempt must be reported as unsuccessful. An affidavit or certificate of service can only truthfully describe the server's own experience or the specific fictional activity expressly permitted by the assessor. Court rules differ between jurisdictions, individuals, companies, family matters and overseas procedures.

BSBESB305 focuses on identifying compliance obligations and turning professional advice into workable procedures for a new business venture. Record the relevant licence, tax, insurance, privacy, workplace and contractual requirements using current sources and the circumstances of the proposed enterprise. Get qualified advice where needed. Assign responsibility, test procedures, gather feedback and preserve evidence of what was actually implemented. A fictional professional reply should never be represented as an actual consultation.

BSBESB402 extends into business structures, contracts, assets, intellectual property and risk. Identify, assess and treat at least three distinct business risks. A practical supplier contract needs the agreed service, payment, data rights, delivery, responsibilities and termination terms. Compare leasing and ownership accurately; paying for software does not necessarily grant ownership of it. Treat risks with actual controls and review points, not colourful spreadsheets alone. Insurance may transfer some financial consequences but does not remove legal or safety duties.

The common lesson is evidence of performance. A study note may prepare you to use a form, but the assessor needs the actual performance evidence. Use genuine work or explicitly approved simulations, with time, person, source, approval and outcome clearly distinguished. Check the current training register and Academy assessment before making any declaration about what you did.
