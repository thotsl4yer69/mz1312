THE INVESTIGATION DESK. Foundation repair one. Working effectively in investigative services. CPPINV3026.

A private investigator receives a workplace allegation, a camera export, a staff roster and unrelated medical information. The client's instruction is to prove the worker exaggerated an injury. That is not a neutral investigation question. Before starting, define the client's authorised purpose, the activity permitted by your licence, the methods agreed in the instructions and what would count as reliable factual evidence. Separate a reported claim from a direct observation and a conclusion. Do not treat your qualification as conferring police powers to compel answers or obtain confidential records.

The first major issue is legal authority. Private investigators must check current licensing and recording laws for the jurisdiction and the proposed activity. New South Wales private investigator licensing uses the Class 2E category, not the older Class 1 wording still present in some study notes. A client may agree to pay for a search without having lawful power to authorise every contact, access or recording the investigator proposes.

The second issue is privacy. The Australian Privacy Principles, or APPs, do not automatically bind every small business in the same way. Check whether the particular organisation or activity is covered by the Privacy Act. Where the APPs apply, Principle 1 addresses transparent privacy management. Principle 2 concerns anonymity or pseudonymity. Principle 3 covers solicited personal information. Principle 4 deals with unsolicited information. Principle 5 concerns notice of collection. Principle 6 regulates use and disclosure. Principle 7 addresses direct marketing. Principle 8 concerns overseas disclosures. Principle 9 regulates government-related identifiers. Principle 10 is information quality. Principle 11 requires appropriate security and retention decisions. Principle 12 covers access requests. Principle 13 concerns correction. The received medical spreadsheet does not become fair game simply because the client attached it. Secure the material, minimise access and decide how to handle it through the correct requirements.

The third issue is rights and professional fairness. A person under investigation retains interests in privacy, reputation and proper process. A voluntary witness can decline a conversation. Ask open questions and record what was observed, what was reported second-hand and what remains uncertain. In employment matters, procedural fairness depends on the applicable governing process and the stage of the inquiry. Do not call an allegation proved before testing reasonable alternative explanations.

Evidence continuity is practical, not a slogan. For an original camera export, record who supplied it, the date, the device or system, time offset, file identifier and method of transfer. Preserve the received original. Make marked working copies. Keep an exhibit register showing access and handovers. If a gap in continuity exists, describe it accurately; never invent a missing signature. A neat register does not by itself guarantee admissibility.

Working alone creates additional hazards. Plan lawful access, personal safety, a check-in contact, an escalation procedure if a check-in is missed, and safe withdrawal. A client deadline does not justify unsafe work. Keep professional relationships respectful, confirm changing instructions and service scope, handle disputes through the appropriate process and seek specialist support outside your competence. Mentoring, professional groups and online networking can improve skills, but never publish case records or identifying client details to a profile.

For the assessment, CPPINV3026 requires work involving three distinct case types: general insurance, compulsory third party liability and workers compensation. For each, document the actual relevant work and how legislative requirements apply in the operating jurisdiction and one additional jurisdiction. A hypothetical rehearsal can teach the structure; genuine or expressly approved simulated evidence is required for assessed personal performance.

Five closing questions. What is authorised? Who may be affected? Which laws and privacy duties actually apply? Can another investigator reconstruct the source trail? And what did you personally do rather than merely describe?
