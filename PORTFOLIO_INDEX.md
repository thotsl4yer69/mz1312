## Maturity vocabulary

Use these terms consistently across repositories:

- **Concept** — architecture/design only.
- **Software prototype** — runnable code, incomplete real-world integration.
- **Bench validated** — exercised against the intended physical hardware on a bench.
- **Hardware-integrated prototype** — running on target hardware with documented limitations.
- **Deployed prototype** — installed in its intended environment but still under active hardening.
- **Production** — only when acceptance criteria, deployment, recovery, security and repeatable verification justify the claim.
- **Archived** — historical/superseded; no active development.

## What not to infer from this account

Repository count is not product count. Forks and templates are not authored work. Exposure to C++, Rust, Go, RF, cryptography or ML infrastructure does not imply senior specialist status in those fields. The strongest through-line is **systems integration and rapid technical prototyping across unfamiliar boundaries**.
